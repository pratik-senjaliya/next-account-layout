1. Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)
Focus: Predicting the future through data-driven modeling.
Budgeting & Rolling Forecasts:
Task: Developing annual "bottom-up" budgets aligned with departmental goals.
Task: Creating 12-month rolling forecasts that adapt as market conditions change.
Task: Micro-Task: Performing Variance Analysis (Budget vs. Actual) to explain why targets were missed or exceeded.
Scenario & "What-If" Modeling:
Task: Building dynamic models to test the impact of strategic moves (e.g., What happens if we increase prices by 5%?).
Task: Hiring models to determine the ROI and "break-even" point of a new sales representative or developer.
Cash Flow Management:
Task: Creating 13-Week Cash Flow Projections to manage short-term liquidity and avoid "cash crunches."
Task: Analyzing the "Cash Conversion Cycle" to speed up collections and optimize vendor payments.

2. Strategic Advisory & Decision Support
Focus: Acting as the "Right Hand" to the CEO/Owner.
Profitability & Margin Analysis:
Task: Product/Service Line Profitability: Identifying which offerings are high-margin and which are "money-losers."
Task: Customer Profitability: Calculating the cost to serve specific clients vs. the revenue they generate.
Break-Even & Pricing Strategy:
Task: Calculating the precise revenue point where the business covers all fixed and variable costs.
Task: Analyzing competitor pricing and market trends to advise on "Optimal Price" positioning.
Board & Stakeholder Presentations:
Task: Preparing monthly "Executive Summary" decks for Board meetings or investors.
Task: Translating complex financial data into simple, actionable narratives for non-financial founders.

3. Business Intelligence (BI) & KPI Dashboards
Focus: Real-time visibility into the health of the business.
Custom Dashboard Development:
Task: Building live dashboards using Power BI, Tableau, or Fathom that pull data directly from the ERP.
Task: Tracking Non-Financial KPIs (e.g., website traffic vs. sales conversion, customer churn rates).
Industry-Specific Metrics:
Task: For SaaS: Tracking MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue), LTV (Lifetime Value), and CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost).
Task: For E-commerce: Tracking ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) and Cart Abandonment impact on revenue.
Benchmarking:
Task: Comparing the client’s financial ratios (Gross Margin, Operating Margin) against industry averages.

4. Capital Structure & M&A Support
Focus: Helping the business find and manage money.
Fundraising Readiness:
Task: Preparing "Pro-Forma" financial statements required for Bank Loans or Venture Capital.
Task: Managing the "Financial Data Room" for potential investors during the due diligence process.
Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A):
Task: Buy-side Due Diligence: Reviewing the target company’s books to find "red flags."
Task: Sell-side Preparation: Cleaning up financials to maximize the company’s valuation before a sale.
Capital Allocation:
Task: Advising on whether to reinvest profits into R&D, pay off debt, or distribute dividends.

5. Fractional Controller & Compliance Oversight
Focus: Strengthening the internal financial "backbone."
Internal Control Enhancement:
Task: Implementing "Check and Balance" procedures to prevent fraud and embezzlement.
Task: Developing a "Month-End Close Checklist" to ensure books are closed in under 5 days.
Audit & Tax Liaison:
Task: Leading the relationship with external auditors and tax CPAs to ensure a smooth year-end.
Task: Ensuring the company stays compliant with ASC 606 (Revenue Recognition) and other US GAAP standards.
